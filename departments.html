Specialized Care for Every Kind of Companion.

Because a bird's needs are entirely different from a cat's, our hospital is divided into expert departments.

Canine Unit

Dogs Department

From playful puppies to wise senior dogs, our Canine Unit offers comprehensive care tailored to your dog's breed and life stage. We have specialized exam rooms designed to minimize stress and plenty of treats on hand!

  • Advanced Orthopedics & Joint Care
  • Behavioral Consultations
  • Senior Dog Arthritis Management
Feline Unit

Cats Department

Cats require a delicate touch and a quiet environment. Our cat-friendly practice features separate entrances and exam rooms, ensuring your feline friend feels safe and calm away from the sights and sounds of dogs.

  • Feline-Specific Wellness Exams
  • Thyroid and Kidney Disease Management
  • Fear-Free Cat Handling
Avian Unit

Birds Department

Birds are masters at hiding illness, requiring specialized diagnostic techniques. Our Avian Unit is led by board-certified specialists who understand the unique biology, nutrition, and environmental needs of companion birds.

  • Beak, Nail, and Wing Trims
  • Nutritional Counseling for Parrots
  • Avian Endoscopy and Radiography
Small Mammals Unit

Rabbits Department

Rabbits have highly specialized digestive and dental systems. Our team is trained to identify and treat common rabbit ailments swiftly, from GI stasis to dental malocclusions, in a quiet, predator-free environment.

  • Routine Dental Filings
  • GI Stasis Emergency Care
  • Safe Spay/Neuter Anesthesia Protocols
Exotics Unit

Exotic Pets Department

Reptiles, amphibians, and unique small mammals require very specific husbandry and care. Our Exotics Unit offers specialized blood testing, imaging, and husbandry consulting for your scaled or unique companions.

  • Reptile Parasite Screening
  • Husbandry and Enclosure Consulting
  • Exotics Emergency Support

Preventive Wellness

Across all departments, we emphasize proactive care to catch potential health issues before they become serious.

Emergency Support

No matter the species, if an emergency strikes, our team is equipped to provide life-saving stabilization and care.
